#Overview:

Developed a Power BI dashboard to analyze BMW sales performance across regions, time periods, and product categories. 
The project aimed to identify revenue trends, top-performing markets, and key growth opportunities in the automotive sector.

#Key Responsibilities:

-> Collected and structured BMW sales dataset.
-> Analyzed sales trends by region, model, and time period.
-> Designed interactive dashboards with KPIs and slicers.
-> Visualized revenue growth, product performance, and regional contribution.
-> Identified seasonal patterns and market performance gaps.

#Skills & Tools:

Power BI, Excel, SQL, DAX, Data Visualization

#Key Highlights from Analysis:

-> Certain regions contributed significantly to total revenue.
-> Premium models generated higher revenue despite lower sales volume.
-> Seasonal trends influenced monthly sales performance.
-> SUV and luxury segments showed consistent growth.
-> Regional sales variations highlighted market expansion opportunities.
